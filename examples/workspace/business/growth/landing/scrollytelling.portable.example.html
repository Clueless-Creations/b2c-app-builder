How an unowned exception becomes a verified record

The reading sequence carries the complete explanation. The stage only reinforces which state is under discussion.

  1. 01

    Situation

    The exception arrives without an accountable owner.

    The intake record exists, but nobody is yet responsible for resolving it and its supporting evidence has not been checked.

  2. 02

    Mechanism

    Validation checks ownership and the required evidence.

    The workflow rejects an incomplete handoff, assigns the accountable review group, and records which evidence is still missing.

  3. 03

    Outcome

    The exception moves into a visible review queue.

    The responsible group can now see the unresolved record, its evidence status, and the action required to close it.

  4. 04

    Proof

    The audit trail records who reviewed what and when.

    The completed record names the owner, confirms the evidence check, and preserves the review event for later verification.
