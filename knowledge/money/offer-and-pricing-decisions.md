# Offer And Pricing Decisions

Use this method when research proposes an offer or a selected monetization workflow locks a
production price. A pricing hypothesis is useful research input. It is not an approved price.

## Propose an offer

State the audience, problem, outcome, entitlement, candidate amount, currency and billing period.
Label the amount as a hypothesis. Compare relevant alternatives and their actual offer terms,
not just which price feels opposite to an unpopular incumbent. Do not infer willingness to pay
from category revenue, positive comments, or a competitor's nominal price alone.

Record the test contract, dated exposure and response, limits and decision in
`strategy/OFFER_TEST.md`. Keep observations distinct from inference. A current test may be
inconclusive. A documented founder waiver records residual risk; it is not invented evidence.

## Lock a production price

Require the current offer evidence and an exact founder pricing decision. The opening mandate to
build an app does not authorize setting its production price. Keep amount, currency, billing period,
trial and renewal terms, entitlement, target environment and effective date aligned with the
approved action. Preserve the host's existing legal/pricing and spending authority checks.

A canonical `class.price` instance denotes an accepted commercial decision. Keep proposed prices
in the research hypothesis and decision material until approved. `check:product-md` and the active
revenue gate validate offer evidence for approved pricing. Guidance, a template, or a validator pass
never grants permission to create a product or change a provider's prices.

## Continue without inventing a decision

Missing evidence or authority leaves the price lock pending. Continue independent research,
design and local verification that does not depend on a locked price. Do not install a hard paywall
or promise paid terms as final while the offer is still a hypothesis.
