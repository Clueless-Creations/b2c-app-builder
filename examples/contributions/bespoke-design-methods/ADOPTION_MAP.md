# Bespoke Design Methods: Adoption Map

## Evidence and dispositions

All seven video sources were read as transcripts with corresponding subtitle cues. No video bytes or visual demonstrations were inspected. TXT line ranges below identify the source precisely. Display time ranges are **rounded summaries of subtitle intervals**, not exact cue timestamps or chapter boundaries; V04 intervals overlap where a cue covers several lines. Full transcript fingerprints are in contribution.yaml.

The method decision column preserves separate adaptation, coverage, duplication, context, rejection and evidence-deferral decisions for each unit. Video `contribution.yaml` dispositions use `reference` for ideas informing original synthesis because expression reuse rights are unknown; this is not permission to adapt the transcript prose. Context-only units have `selection: reference-only` and no operative rule. Rejected assertions stay excluded even when another part of their unit informs a method.

Source demonstrations, promotions, biography, historical interpretations and cognitive claims never establish product outcomes. The product must supply the concrete verification named per row.

## Repository methods

Repository source adaptations retain their own licenses and immutable revisions. The following units refine the two aggregate manifest units; they are not separate runtime owners.

| Unit | Selector | Existing responsibility | Transfer, exclusions and proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1. Reference deconstruction | Brand skill, Stage 1. | **Adapt**, R/D. | Preserve transferable principles, source-specific exclusions and product translation. Add source/modality evidence. Test whether the candidate reflects a reasoned relationship rather than copied identity. |
