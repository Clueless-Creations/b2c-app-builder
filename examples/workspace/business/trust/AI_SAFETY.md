# AI Safety

Applicability verdict: unknown
Paid generation: unknown

Any product with generative or conversational AI needs a named self-harm and crisis response: detect self-harm, suicide, or crisis language in user input, and respond with a real crisis resource instead of a generic refusal or an ordinary completion. Test this path explicitly and record the detection method, the response copy, and the escalation owner as a row below.

| Risk or prohibited use | Control | Owner | Test evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| self-harm / suicide / crisis language in user input | detect crisis language and respond with a crisis resource, not a generic refusal or ordinary completion; escalate to a human when the model provider's own safety layer does not already cover this | replace with owner | replace with test evidence | unknown |
