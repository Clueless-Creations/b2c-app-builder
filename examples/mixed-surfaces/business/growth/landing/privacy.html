Privacy

What this fixture stores, who can read it, and how you delete it.

What is stored

A receipt record holds the photograph you upload, the merchant name, the date, the amount, and the folder you chose. The waitlist form on the conversion page stores only the email address you submit.

Who can read it

Only the signed-in owner of the account can read that account’s receipts. This fixture does not sell receipt images or waitlist addresses.

How to delete

Email privacy@example.invalid from the address on the account. Deletion removes the photographs, the parsed fields, and the waitlist row. This page has no signup form and no scroll-linked story.
