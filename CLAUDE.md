4. Use MCP/CLI according to the capability and authority actually exposed. Do not infer local execution, hosted execution, provider readiness, or write authority from a connection/server name.
5. For app repositories generated by this project, follow that workspace's `AGENTS.md`; do not copy this maintainer adapter into the workspace.
