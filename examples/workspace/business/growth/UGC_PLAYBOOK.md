## Post-Breakout Scale Model

Discovery (3-5 creators) is not the destination. Once the breakout bar holds (2-3 hits from the same structure across 2+ creators with downstream install/paywall evidence), the roster grows in bands per `ugc-creator-engine.md` — each band a separate founder-gated budget step with the prior band's install-per-video and payback numbers in front of the decision.
