From an unowned slip to a found receipt

Four states show a paper slip with no owner, a photograph of its fields, a filed record, and a later search result.

  1. Situation. The slip sits in a bag with no date, merchant, or owner.
  2. Mechanism. One photograph records the merchant, date, and amount.
  3. Outcome. The receipt is stored under that merchant and week.
  4. Proof. A later search returns the same receipt, not a look-alike.
