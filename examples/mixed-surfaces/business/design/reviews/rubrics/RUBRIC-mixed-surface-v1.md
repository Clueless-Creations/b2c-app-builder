# RUBRIC-mixed-surface-v1

Frozen before the independent review. Do not edit after seeing a candidate.
A change is a new version with a stated reason.

## Surfaces

Score each page against its studio `interaction` class in
`studio/seed/business.json`.

| Surface | Required | Not required merely by being a public page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `privacy` (`static-document`) | Semantic content, legibility, working links, accurate claims | Scroll choreography, cinematic imagery, conversion experiments |
| `conversion` (`conversion`) | Promise, one first useful action, truthful waitlist copy | Scroll-linked animation because the cinematic page uses it |
| `cinematic` (`scroll-linked`) | Situation to mechanism to outcome to proof, readable without JavaScript, reduced-motion still complete | Fashionable motion that does not change comprehension |

## Verdict rule

Evaluate actual fit and usability. The mere presence of fashionable effects
is not a pass. A privacy page that stays a document is not a fail. A
cinematic page whose motion hides the story is a fail.
