# grug: carry the mechanism through the product

Source: [Apple's developer interview, 2026-05-16](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ux44ymcr).
Read on 2026-09-04. This is a documentation observation, not a recorded interaction.

The team describes a custom stroke engine extending to layout, animation, sizing,
icons, and interaction states. The transfer to TUCK is consistency of mechanism:
the original packed objects, bag, labels, forms, and state changes must belong to
one product. A generic list behind a distinctive hero does not meet that standard.
The interview also supports a deliberately small offline product without adding
accounts simply to resemble a larger service. TUCK keeps its own words and drawings.
